1. Copy your game images into THIS folder (next to index.html). See MISSING-IMAGES.txt.
2. Open a terminal in this folder and run:
       python server.py
   (on Windows you can also try:  py server.py)
3. Open http://localhost:8000 in your browser.
4. Press Ctrl+C in the terminal to stop.

Accounts and progress are stored in qurridor.db (created automatically). Back it up; don't delete it.
